How it works

1

Tell us what happened

In your own words — no legal or union jargon needed. Upload letters or documents if you have them.

2

Get a clear explanation

Our AI assistant organises your story, explains what the situation appears to be in plain English, and shows the sources it used. It is clearly labelled and never pretends to be Kelly.

3

Kelly reviews

When it matters — or when you ask — Kelly personally reviews your case and sends you a checked response and action plan.

We support NHS staff across the UK. We are not a law firm and do not provide guaranteed legal advice; we help you understand your situation and prepare, with human review.

Honest about AI

AI helps organise facts, explain guidance and prepare summaries — always labelled, always source-backed where possible, and never a barrier between you and human help. Read our AI transparency statement.

Band reviews & fair pay

Think your job has outgrown its band? Our dedicated band review service walks you through the evidence step by step — your job description, what you actually do, what has changed — and Kelly personally reviews every part before anything is produced.

You get a plain-English report with clear next steps, and a formal review request ready to send to your employer. Indicative and evidence-based: only your employer's job evaluation panel can decide a band.

Pricing and pilot access

Health Staff Advisory is currently in a private pilot. Accounts are free during the pilot, and every case gets the same safety-first handling. Paid membership tiers will be introduced after the pilot — pilot members will be told well in advance.

Contact

Questions about the service or the pilot? Send us a message and we will reply — in your Inbox if you have an account, or by a private link if you do not. Signed-in members should raise case questions inside their case so nothing gets lost.
